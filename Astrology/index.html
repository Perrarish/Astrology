Eight Sacred Directions

Vasthu Shastra Tips

01
Main Door Facing East

The entrance should ideally face east (Indra's direction) to welcome the morning sun's energy, bringing health and auspiciousness to the household.

02
Kitchen in South-East

Agni (fire) governs the south-east corner — the ideal placement for the kitchen. The cook should face east while preparing food.

03
Master Bedroom South-West

The south-west zone, governed by Nirrti, represents stability and strength — perfect for the master bedroom, ensuring deep sleep and strong relationships.

04
Pooja Room North-East

Ishanya (north-east) is the most sacred corner — seat of the divine. A pooja room here amplifies spiritual energy throughout the home.

05
Water in North or East

Underground tanks, wells, and water features should be in the north or north-east to maintain positive water element energy and financial flow.
